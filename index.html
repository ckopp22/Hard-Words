      How to Play

      Be the fastest to shout a word that starts with the first letter of the image.

      1. Add everyone playing, then pick your categories.
      2. When someone shouts a right answer, tap their name — they get a point and a new picture appears.
      3. Nobody can think of one? Tap the picture to skip to the next.
      4. Tap End Round when you're done to see who won.
