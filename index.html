      How to Play

      1. Add everyone playing, then pick your categories.
      2. Hold the screen so the describer can see the word (and nobody else).
      3. Describe or act out the word without saying the obvious clues.
      4. When someone guesses it, tap their name — they get a point and a new word appears.
      5. Stuck? Tap the word to skip to the next one.
      6. Tap End Round when you're done to see who won.
